# Changelog

The release notes for every version. `scripts/release_notes.sh <version>` prints one version's section. The release
tag's message and the GitHub release text both come from it, and the apps show that text when they offer the update.
Keep each section plain text, one line per `-` bullet, with no links or formatting: the apps show it as is.

## 0.1.0

The first public release, for Android, Windows, Linux and macOS (Apple silicon).

- Study: Today with the next class, the rest of the day and what is due within seven days; courses, the week and semester calendar, coursework from Courseware, Vips and submission folders, messages, forums, participants, announcements, wiki and Courseware reading, office-hour booking and calendar export (.ics).
- Course files: open, save a copy, or sync a whole course into a folder on the device. Uploads to submission folders ask for confirmation first.
- Library: catalogue search with copies and shelf locations, saved books, loans, reservations, fees, renewals and reminders.
- Mensa: every Studierendenwerk Augsburg location by day or week, with diet and allergen filters, prices for your group, favourite dishes and English translations.
- Five home-screen widgets on Android: Your day, Next class, Mensa today, Due soon and Library.
- Saved sign-ins: keep university and library sign-ins in the device's secure storage and fill them on the official sign-in pages. The Authenticator step stays on your phone.
- Works offline with what you have already opened. Updates install from GitHub on Android, Windows and Linux; macOS links to the new release.
- Not signed yet: Windows shows a SmartScreen prompt once, and macOS needs Open Anyway in Privacy & Security once.
